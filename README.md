# PISB R&D Wing

The research and development wing of the **PICT IEEE Student Branch (PISB)**. Its goal is a research-driven culture at PICT, in which first-, second- and third-year students build technical depth, work on real projects together, and take part in global IEEE programmes such as EPICS, HAC and Tech4Good.

## 2026 plan at a glance

| Quarter | Focus | Key activities |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** (Jan–Mar) | Foundations and orientation | Research orientation bootcamp (literature review, documentation, reproducibility) · research circles in ML/AI, IoT, cybersecurity and sustainability · expert talks by IIT/IISc/PICT alumni |
| **Q2** (Apr–Jun) | Project kickoff and proposals | Launch 2–3 R&D projects (AI / IoT / sustainability) · internal call and mentoring for IEEE proposals (EPICS, Tech4Good) · Green Labs and e-waste reuse drive |
| **Q3** (Jul–Sep) | Development and collaborations | Model building and prototyping · mentorship calls with IIT/IISc research scholars · industry research talk |
| **Q4** (Oct–Dec) | Review and submissions | Internal milestone review · final IEEE proposal submissions (EPICS, HAC, Tech4Good) · sustainability annual summary |

**Roles by year:**
- **First years:** documentation, surveys, prototyping help, event execution
- **Second years:** project ownership, proposal writing, domain leadership
- **Third years:** guidance, architecture, review, external collaborations

## Writing a paper

- [`paper-writing/IEEE-Conference-Template.pdf`](paper-writing/IEEE-Conference-Template.pdf) is the standard IEEE two-column conference format. LaTeX and Word sources are on IEEE's [conference templates page](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html), and Overleaf has the LaTeX version ready to use.
- Write the related-work section first, and keep a shared BibTeX file from day one.

## Contributing

Add each year's plan as `plans/rnd-plan-<year>.pdf`. Add new opportunities as `opportunities/<programme>-<year>/`, with the rules and any concept note. **Do not commit** registration sheets or anything with members' phone numbers or emails.
