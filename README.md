# PISB R&D Wing

The research and development wing of the **PICT IEEE Student Branch (PISB)**. It helps students go past coursework: read papers, run small research projects, and write them up.

## Current initiatives

- **MRL shared task (EMNLP 2026):** PISB members are building question-answering datasets in five Devanagari-script languages (Sanskrit, Marwari, Marathi, Hindi, Bhojpuri) for the shared task at the *Workshop on Multilingual Representation Learning*.
- **EPICS in IEEE proposal drafting:** teams have drafted several community-engineering proposals: dyslexia screening, wearable gas-leak detection, sanitation-worker safety, flood monitoring, and the Kisaan Mitra / CAAFAS → AgroSaarthi farming assistant. All are archived in [`epics-in-ieee`](https://github.com/PICT-IEEE-Student-Branch/epics-in-ieee).

## 2026 plan at a glance

| Quarter | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Q1** (Jan–Mar) | Research orientation bootcamp (literature review, documentation, reproducibility) · research circles in ML/AI, IoT, cybersecurity and sustainability · alumni and expert talks |
| **Q2** (Apr–Jun) | Kick off 2–3 student research projects |
| **Q3** (Jul–Sep) | Build and prototype · mentorship calls with research scholars · an industry research talk |
| **Q4** (Oct–Dec) | Internal review of projects and write-ups |

**Roles by year:** first years document and prototype, second years own projects and domains, and third years guide and review.

## Writing a paper

- [`paper-writing/IEEE-Conference-Template.pdf`](paper-writing/IEEE-Conference-Template.pdf) is the standard IEEE two-column format. LaTeX and Word sources are on IEEE's [conference templates page](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html), and Overleaf has the LaTeX version ready to use.
- Write the related-work section first, and keep a shared BibTeX file from day one.
